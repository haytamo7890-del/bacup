Diem → Bac-up inspiration

Diem is a pro video / RAW media app. Its brand is electric arctic blue on warm cinematic dark — which maps almost perfectly onto Bac-up's arctic-glass. Below: the real palette pulled from the binary, and how to adapt each idea (not copy) to the landing.

The signature — a glowing gradient orb

One radiant focal object

Diem's whole identity is a single multi-stop radial gradient — mint→cyan→blue→deep-navy with a white hot-spot and a soft teal bloom around it. It reads as "light / energy" against warm charcoal.

For Bac-up: a slow-rotating arctic orb behind the hero headline (or as the "B" mark's halo) gives instant premium tech feel with zero illustration work.

Warm dark neutrals (the un-obvious part)

Diem doesn't use pure grey/black. The darks are warm (green-tinged charcoal) and the lights are cream. That warmth is what makes the blue glow "pop" instead of looking like a generic SaaS dark theme.
